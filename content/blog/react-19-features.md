Motion works best when it answers a question: what changed, where did it go, and what can I do next?

## Preserve continuity

Small transitions between states help users understand that content has transformed rather than disappeared. Compose makes these moments approachable, but restraint keeps them useful.

## Tune the feeling

Duration is only part of the result. Easing, distance, and hierarchy determine whether an interaction feels brisk, calm, or distracting. Test animations on real hardware and respect reduced-motion preferences.
